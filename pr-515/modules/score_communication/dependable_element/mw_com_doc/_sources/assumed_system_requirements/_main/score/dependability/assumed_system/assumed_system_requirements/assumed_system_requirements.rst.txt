Assumed System Requirements
===========================
.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_ErrorHandling

   (C++) exceptions shall not be used for error handling.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_InterProcessCommunication

   The platform shall support inter-process communication.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_IntraProcessCommunication

   The platform shall support intra-process communication.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_ExternalCommunicationSomeip

   The platform shall support external communication via SOME/IP protocol.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_SafeCommunication

   The platform shall support safe communication in terms of ISO 26262. I.e. it shall provide mechanisms to detect communication errors.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_SecureCommunication

   The information exchanged between applications shall be protected against unauthorized access and manipulation. The platform shall provide mechanisms to ensure confidentiality, integrity, and authenticity of the exchanged information.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_SupportForPollingBasedArchitectures

   The platform shall support a deterministic, polling-based application execution model that triggers logic based on predefined intervals.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_SupportForDataDrivenArchitecture

   The platform shall support an event-driven application architecture where execution is triggered by data changes.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_SupportForRequestDrivenArchitecture

   The platform shall support a request-driven application architecture that processes requests on-demand.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_ProgrammingLanguagesForApplicationDevelopment

   The platform shall support implementation of applications in the following programming languages up to the ASIL-B level: C++, Rust.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_TracingOfCommunication

   The platform shall support the tracing of communication events for internal and external communication systems.

.. requirement:definition:: Communication.ASR_AvailabilityOfCommunicationPartners

   The platform shall provide a mechanism to detect the availability of communication partners for internal and external communication systems and provide the required information to communicate with them.
